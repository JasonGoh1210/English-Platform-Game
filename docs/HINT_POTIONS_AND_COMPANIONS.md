# Starlight hint potions and ten companions

The shop sells a30Coin Starlight Hint Potion alongside the25Coin Ruby Potion (updated2026-10-17; existing bottles are retained). Separate server-owned inventory counts preserve old purchases with an implicit0 hint bottles; no SQL migration is needed. Each type has its own99-bottle cap. Use during normal or timed battle questions; each clue requires the current question token, an active unpaused battle and an unexpired question. At most one bottle per question. Existing transactional receipts protect request retries. Clues do not submit answers, change HP, charge extra Coins, or award accuracy/rewards. The clue survives refresh and clears on the next question. Castle spelling retains its original hints. See [current battle supply and mistake rules](BATTLE_SUPPLIES_AND_MISTAKES.md).

Judgment questions provide bilingual context examples from data/question_hints.json. Odd-word questions rule out one ordinary option without removing the correct answer. Sentence questions reveal at most three opening words, leaving at least two to arrange. Clues are absent from public question responses until an owned potion is used.
